Stud Contract — Locked Rules & Decisions
1. Contract architecture

A stud owner publishes one active set of stud terms for a dog.

There are not multiple selectable compensation offers under one listing.

The published terms define the conditions under which the dog is offered to outside bitches.

When a bitch owner proceeds with those terms and the required approval occurs, an individual breeding contract is created for that sire/dam pairing.

Once accepted, the individual contract is immutable for that breeding. Later edits to the public stud listing do not change an already accepted contract.

This immutability refers to preserving agreement terms. It is unrelated to frozen semen or semen storage.

2. Compensation

The contract supports exactly three compensation structures:

Cash
Puppy Back
Cash + Puppy Back

No separate Deposit + Puppy type is required in the initial system.

The contract may obligate the dam owner to provide at most one puppy.

There are no multiple compensation options within one published stud offer.

3. Puppy-back pick position

The sire owner may require:

First Pick
Second Pick

Third pick and higher are not supported.

Pick position means selection order, not an objective ranking of puppy quality.

First Pick

The stud owner makes the first contractual selection from the qualifying litter.

If the contract also specifies a sex, the stud owner may select only from puppies of that sex.

Example:

First Pick Female

The stud owner makes the first selection, but only from available females.

Second Pick

The dam owner receives the first protected selection.

After the dam owner selects—or forfeits that selection—the stud owner's selection turn begins.

Example:

Second Pick Female

Dam owner selects one puppy of either sex.
Stud owner's turn opens.
Stud owner may select only from the remaining females.

The dam owner's first pick is not sex-restricted by the stud contract.

If the dam owner selects the only female, the stud owner's second-pick female obligation may become impossible to fulfill. The game does not modify the agreement to compensate for that outcome.

4. Puppy sex requirement

Whenever Puppy Back is part of compensation, the stud owner must explicitly choose:

Either
Male
Female

This is a requirement, not a preference.

If the required sex is unavailable when the stud owner's selection turn occurs, the game does not substitute another sex.

Example:

Second Pick Female

If no female remains when the stud owner's turn begins:

the puppy-back portion cannot be fulfilled;
a male cannot be substituted automatically;
no cash substitution occurs;
the contract is not treated as erroneous;
return-service consequences depend only on the independently configured return-service conditions.
5. Puppy-selection eligibility window

Puppy selection takes place during the puppies' first eight weeks of life.

The neonatal mortality window closes at the end of Week 1.

Therefore the normal contractual puppy-selection period begins:

Day 8

and must be resolved before puppies become normally eligible for rehoming at:

8 weeks of age / Day 56

The operational selection turns occur near the beginning of this window rather than remaining indefinitely open.

First-pick contract

After the Week 1 neonatal window closes:

stud owner's selection turn opens;
stud owner has 24 real hours to make the selection.
Second-pick contract

After the Week 1 neonatal window closes:

First 24-hour window

dam owner makes their first protected selection.

If the dam owner selects:

stud owner's second-pick turn opens immediately.

If the dam owner does not select within 24 real hours:

dam owner's protected first-pick right is forfeited;
no puppy is automatically selected for them;
stud owner's second-pick turn opens.

Second 24-hour window

stud owner has up to 24 real hours to make their contractual selection.

This produces a maximum normal selection sequence of approximately 48 real hours after the Week 1 neonatal window.

There is no third-pick contract because the system deliberately supports only these two selection positions.

6. Missed puppy-selection deadlines

The game never automatically chooses a puppy for either kennel.

Player-facing contract language should explicitly state:

Failure to exercise a puppy-selection right before its deadline forfeits that selection right.

Dam owner misses first-pick deadline

For a Second Pick contract:

dam owner's protected first-pick right is forfeited;
the stud owner's turn begins;
the game does not select a puppy for the dam owner.
Stud owner misses selection deadline
puppy-back right is forfeited;
no puppy is assigned;
no automatic cash substitution occurs;
no penalty payment is generated solely because the stud owner failed to select;
no return service is generated solely because the stud owner failed to select.

After the puppy-back obligation is resolved or forfeited, related contract holds can be released as appropriate.

7. Contract protection of the litter

While a contractual selection right remains unresolved:

Every puppy that could legally satisfy an outstanding contractual selection position remains contract-protected.

Protected puppies cannot be:

sold;
rehomed;
transferred;
removed from the game;
named.

This prevents either kennel from altering the available selection pool before required picks are completed.

Once all required selections are made or forfeited, unnecessary protections are released.

This is deliberately aligned with the normal rule that puppies cannot be rehomed before 8 weeks.

8. Selected puppy dies before transfer

If the stud owner has selected a contract puppy and that puppy dies before transfer:

If the contractual selection window is still open

Reopen the stud owner's selection using the remaining qualifying puppies.

The original contract rules remain unchanged:

same pick position;
same sex requirement;
same underlying agreement.

The contract is not rewritten.

If no qualifying replacement exists

Then:

the puppy-back portion cannot be fulfilled;
no automatic cash substitution occurs;
no contract error occurs;
return service is generated only if the contract's independently defined litter-size return-service condition is actually satisfied.

This rule must be explicitly explained in the Stud Contract Worksheet when Puppy Back is selected.

9. Minimum litter size

When Puppy Back is used, the stud owner selects the minimum qualifying litter size.

The definition is:

Minimum litter size = number of surviving puppies at the contract's litter-qualification checkpoint.

The available minimums should be presented as structured options such as:

1+
2+
3+

The worksheet is sequential.

Earlier answers constrain later answers.

For example:

First Pick can permit 1+.
Second Pick cannot logically use 1+ as a viable minimum.

Impossible choices should be disabled and visually explained rather than silently accepted.

If an earlier worksheet answer changes, downstream answers affected by that change should be invalidated and the player returned to the relevant decision point rather than leaving stale incompatible selections in place.

10. Puppy-back failure is not automatically a contract failure

A puppy-back obligation may become impossible without invalidating the contract.

Example:

Cash + Second Pick Female

The litter meets all other contract conditions, but no qualifying female remains at the stud owner's turn.

Result:

cash portion stands;
puppy portion is unfulfilled;
no alternate sex is substituted;
no automatic cash replacement occurs;
contract remains valid;
return-service rules do not change merely because the requested sex was unavailable.

The stud owner accepts this risk when publishing the terms.

11. Return service

Return service is configured by the stud owner in advance.

It is not automatically generated for every unsuccessful contractual outcome.

There are two independent concepts.

No-litter return service

The stud owner chooses whether a return service is provided if the breeding does not produce a litter under the defined breeding lifecycle outcome.

Options:

Offered
Not offered
Small-litter return service

The stud owner may define a surviving-litter threshold.

Available thresholds are:

1 or fewer
2 or fewer
3 or fewer

or no small-litter return service.

Return service is based on the configured surviving litter-size threshold.

It is not triggered merely because:

the requested puppy sex was unavailable;
the dam owner chose a puppy that made the stud owner's requested sex unavailable;
the stud owner missed the selection deadline;
the stud owner declined or forfeited the puppy;
the selected puppy died unless the independently configured litter-size condition itself is satisfied.

The contract remains immutable; the game does not rewrite the guarantee after seeing the litter outcome.

12. Dam requirements

Initial Stud Contract dam requirements are deliberately limited to requirements the game can evaluate authoritatively.

Per-test health requirements: Every canonical health test made available to the Stud Contract worksheet is configured independently. For each test, the stud owner chooses No restriction, Green or Yellow, or Green only. No restriction means that test is ignored for Stud Contract qualification and no completed result is required. Green or Yellow requires a completed current result of Green or Yellow for that specific test. Green only requires a completed current Green result for that specific test. A missing result fails only when that specific test has a Green-or-Yellow or Green-only requirement. Results on other tests do not affect that requirement. Adding new canonical health tests later does not change this rule; they simply become additional configurable tests in the worksheet.

Health-status independence: Aggregate health indicators, including any game UI indicator that a dog has completed all tests with Green results, are not Stud Contract eligibility authority. Stud Contract health eligibility is evaluated only from the per-test requirements stored in the contract snapshot.

Titles

The stud owner chooses:

No restriction
CH or higher
GCH or higher

No trait minimum/maximum requirements are part of the initial Stud Contract system.

No free-text eligibility requirements are part of the initial enforceable system.

Normal biological breeding age limits remain game rules rather than owner-defined contractual restrictions.

13. Automatic approval

The stud owner chooses:

Automatic Approval

If the dam satisfies all contractual requirements and both dogs satisfy normal current breeding requirements, the dam owner may confirm the agreement without individual sire-owner intervention.

Publishing an automatically approved stud offer constitutes the sire owner's advance approval of qualifying outside breedings under those terms.

When the dam owner confirms:

current eligibility is rechecked;
the individual contract becomes accepted/immutable;
the normal breeding attempt begins.

A contract does not override ordinary biological or operational availability.

14. Manual approval

The stud owner may instead choose:

Manual Approval

Even when a dam satisfies every published requirement, the sire owner must explicitly approve that breeding.

When the dam owner submits the request:

no breeding attempt is created yet;
the dam enters a temporary conceptual state of Stud approval pending;
the sire owner receives a pending request;
the request has a 24 real-hour response deadline.

The stud owner may:

Approve
Decline

If declined:

request resolves;
the pending contract restriction on the dam disappears;
the dam's actual current breeding eligibility is recalculated.

If no response occurs within 24 real hours:

request expires;
no breeding attempt occurs;
no fee is charged solely for the expired request;
the dam's pending restriction disappears;
her actual current eligibility is recalculated.

She does not blindly become "eligible"; she returns to whatever eligibility her current health, lifecycle, breeding, and ownership state permits.

15. Multiple pending requests for one stud

There is no artificial cap on how many manual-approval requests a stud can receive.

A high-demand sire may accumulate many pending requests.

For example, a stud could have ten pending requests simultaneously.

The stud owner can choose which qualifying request to approve.

Approving one request does not automatically decline the others.

Other requests remain pending until:

individually approved;
individually declined;
or their own 24-hour deadline expires.

This naturally allows highly active stud kennels with many sires to operate as a meaningful game economy.

A centralized Pending Stud Requests management surface will eventually be required, rather than expecting players to manage all requests solely from individual dog pages.

The exact UI for that management page is not yet locked.

16. Pending requests do not reserve the sire

Submitting a manual approval request does not reserve the stud.

The sire remains subject to:

other pending requests;
normal owner use;
other approved breeding activity;
Stud Recovery;
Breeding Availability;
all normal biological and operational restrictions.

The queue is a collection of requests, not a reservation system.

17. Approve is available only when the stud can actually breed

For manual approval:

Approve is actionable only while the sire is currently eligible to perform the breeding.

If he is in Stud Recovery or otherwise temporarily unavailable:

Approve is disabled;
Decline remains available;
request remains pending;
its existing 24-hour request deadline continues to run.

The UI should explain the actual reason.

For Stud Recovery, display the existing countdown/cooldown information rather than merely saying "Unavailable."

Once the sire becomes available, any still-active request may be approved.

Some requests may naturally expire while a heavily used sire is recovering.

That is intentional rather than an artificial queue cap.

18. Stud owner's own breeding use

Outside Stud Contract restrictions apply only to outside stud service.

They must never accidentally prevent the sire owner from using their own dog through the ordinary in-kennel breeding system.

Owner breeding and outside contractual breeding remain separate paths.

19. Stud Recovery remains authoritative

A contract does not override the existing two-hour Stud Recovery system.

Likewise:

a pending request does not reserve future availability;
satisfying dam requirements does not make the sire biologically available;
contract approval cannot bypass recovery.

Stud Recovery remains an ordinary game constraint.

20. Acceptance and immutable agreement

Once both sides have supplied the consent required by the selected approval model, a contract exists for that particular breeding.

Automatic Approval

The sire owner's consent is represented by publishing the automatically approved offer.

Dam owner confirms.

The resulting individual agreement becomes accepted and immutable.

Manual Approval

Dam owner submits the terms.

The requested terms must remain fixed while the approval request is pending.

Sire owner approves those exact terms.

The resulting individual agreement becomes accepted and immutable.

The public Stud Offer can later be edited for future breedings without changing:

existing accepted contracts;
existing pending requests based on earlier terms.

An individual contract is tied to its specific sire and dam.

21. Sequential worksheet behavior

The Stud Owner Worksheet will be step-based.

Later choices depend on earlier choices.

Examples:

Cash compensation does not require puppy-back questions.
Puppy Back exposes pick position and sex.
Second Pick affects valid minimum-litter options.
Changing compensation may invalidate the entire puppy-back section.
Changing pick position may invalidate minimum-litter answers.

When an earlier answer changes:

Invalidate affected downstream decisions and return the worksheet to the relevant decision point.

Do not silently preserve incompatible downstream answers.

This is preferable to attempting complex partial reconciliation.

These are intentionally not part of the current locked rules and should not be inferred during implementation:

# Stud Contract — Whelp-Time Qualification, Puppy Selection Timing, Return Service, and Contract History Addendum

This section supersedes earlier Stud Contract rules concerning:

- Day-8 litter qualification;
- surviving-puppy-count qualification;
- opening Puppy Back selection after the Week 1 neonatal window;
- rolling 24-hour Second Pick deadlines;
- unresolved Return Service credit/lifecycle semantics.

Where an earlier Stud Contract rule conflicts with this addendum, this addendum is authoritative.

---

## 24. Contract formation and compensation settlement

An individual Stud Contract exists only after the acceptance transaction succeeds.

### Automatic Approval

When the dam owner confirms:

1. current eligibility is rechecked;
2. required funds are rechecked;
3. required stud-owner compensation is transferred;
4. the immutable Stud Contract is created;
5. the BreedingAttempt is created.

If the dam owner does not have sufficient funds to satisfy the agreed stud-owner compensation at that moment:

- no accepted Stud Contract is created;
- no BreedingAttempt is created;
- no partial contract remains;
- the transaction fails with clear player-facing text.

The agreement did not later terminate; it never successfully formed.

### Manual Approval

When the stud owner approves:

1. the exact pending immutable terms are reloaded;
2. current eligibility is rechecked;
3. required funds are rechecked;
4. required stud-owner compensation is transferred;
5. the Stud Contract becomes accepted;
6. the BreedingAttempt is created.

If the dam owner cannot fund the agreed compensation at approval time:

- approval cannot complete;
- no accepted contract is created from that approval;
- no BreedingAttempt is created.

### Compensation already paid

When a contract later enters Return Service, any original cash stud compensation has already been fulfilled.

Return Service does not charge the stud-owner compensation a second time.

Ordinary game expenses associated with a new breeding attempt remain separate and may be charged again according to normal game rules.

Examples include applicable:

- veterinary costs;
- testing costs;
- travel costs;
- ordinary breeding/system fees.

---

## 25. Whelp-time litter qualification

No-litter and Small-Litter Return Service outcomes are based on reproductive outcome, not later puppy-selection success.

### Litter-size determination

When a litter is created, the game records the number of puppies born alive.

That live-born count is the permanent contractual litter-size result.

It is used for both:

- Puppy Back minimum qualifying litter;
- Small-Litter Return Service threshold.

Later puppy deaths do not change the contractual litter-size result.

This includes:

- neonatal deaths;
- later puppy deaths;
- death of a selected Puppy Back puppy.

### Neonatal mortality

Neonatal mortality remains part of the biological simulation.

It does not retroactively change:

- the live-born litter count;
- whether the Puppy Back minimum litter requirement was met;
- whether Small-Litter Return Service was triggered.

Example:

Three puppies are born alive.

The contract's Small-Litter Return Service threshold is:

2 or fewer.

At whelping:

- live-born count = 3;
- Small-Litter Return Service is not triggered.

If two puppies later die during neonatal mortality:

- the contractual live-born count remains 3;
- Return Service is not created or revived.

Those deaths are biological outcomes after the contractual litter-size determination.

---

## 26. Puppy Back minimum qualifying litter

When Puppy Back is used, the stud owner selects:

- 1+
- 2+
- 3+

For Second Pick:

- 1+ remains unavailable because two contractual selection positions cannot be satisfied from one puppy.

The definition is now:

**Minimum qualifying litter = number of puppies born alive when the litter is created.**

This supersedes the previous surviving-puppy Day-8 qualification definition.

Puppy Back minimum litter size and Small-Litter Return Service are separate contract terms.

Example:

Puppy Back minimum:

3+

Small-Litter Return Service:

2 or fewer

Two puppies are born alive.

Result:

- Small-Litter Return Service is triggered;
- Puppy Back minimum is not met;
- no Puppy Back selection opens.

Another example:

Puppy Back minimum:

2+

Small-Litter Return Service:

1 or fewer

Two puppies are born alive.

Result:

- no Return Service;
- Puppy Back minimum is met;
- Puppy Back selection opens.

If the required puppy sex later proves unavailable, the Puppy Back portion may remain unfulfilled, but Return Service is not created.

---

## 27. Return Service trigger authority

Return Service protects only the reproductive outcome promised by the contract.

It does not insure successful Puppy Back fulfillment.

Return Service may arise only from:

1. the breeding produces no litter and the immutable contract offered No-Litter Return Service; or
2. the litter's live-born count satisfies the immutable Small-Litter Return Service threshold.

### Puppy Back does not create Return Service

Return Service is not created merely because:

- the requested puppy sex does not exist;
- the dam owner's protected pick consumes the only puppy of the requested sex;
- the stud owner misses a puppy-selection deadline;
- the dam owner misses the protected first-pick deadline;
- the Puppy Back minimum was otherwise actionable but no legal puppy remains later;
- the selected contract puppy dies;
- no qualifying replacement remains;
- a player voluntarily forfeits a selection right.

These are risks of the Puppy Back terms.

The stud owner accepts those risks when publishing the contract.

### Return Service is settled from reproductive outcome

If enough puppies are born alive to avoid the configured Small-Litter Return Service condition:

**the Return Service question for that litter is permanently settled at whelping.**

Later Puppy Back failure cannot create Return Service.

The Puppy Back portion of the contract may still remain unresolved after the Return Service question has been settled.

---

## 28. Puppy-selection eligibility and fixed age-based deadlines

Puppy Back selection begins after the litter is created.

It no longer waits for the Week 1 neonatal mortality window to close.

Players may deliberately wait during their available selection window before choosing a puppy.

The game never automatically chooses a puppy.

### First Pick contract

For a First Pick contract:

- the stud owner's turn becomes actionable after litter creation;
- the fixed deadline is puppy age 24 real hours from whelping.

The deadline is determined from the litter's birth time.

It does not move based on when the player first views the litter.

### Second Pick contract

For a Second Pick contract:

the ordinary selection schedule is fixed from the litter's birth time.

#### Dam owner's protected first pick

The dam owner may select immediately after the litter is created.

Deadline:

**puppy age 24 real hours from whelping**

The dam owner's protected first pick remains unrestricted by the stud owner's puppy-sex requirement.

#### Stud owner's second pick

The stud owner's turn becomes actionable when:

- the dam owner makes the protected first pick; or
- the dam owner's protected first-pick deadline expires and that right is forfeited.

The stud owner's deadline is always:

**puppy age 48 real hours from whelping**

The deadline does not become 24 hours from the moment the dam selects.

Example:

Litter born Monday at noon.

Dam protected first-pick deadline:

Tuesday at noon.

Stud second-pick deadline:

Wednesday at noon.

If the dam chooses Monday at 12:15 PM:

- the stud owner may begin selecting immediately;
- the stud deadline remains Wednesday at noon.

If the dam chooses Tuesday at 11:55 AM:

- the stud owner begins then;
- the stud deadline remains Wednesday at noon.

If the dam does not choose:

- the dam's right is forfeited at Tuesday noon;
- the stud owner's turn opens;
- the stud deadline remains Wednesday noon.

This gives both players a predictable deadline from the moment the litter is born.

### Deadline authority

These ordinary puppy-selection deadlines are age-anchored real-world timestamps calculated from the authoritative litter birth time.

They are persisted exactly.

The game does not continually rebuild deadlines from the current time.

---

## 29. Puppy-selection cutoff and selected-puppy death

The broader contractual puppy-selection/reselection window remains open only before normal rehoming eligibility at the beginning of Week 8 / Day 56.

This outer boundary is separate from the ordinary First Pick and Second Pick deadlines.

### Ordinary picks

Normal First/Second Pick rights use the fixed 24-hour / 48-hour age-based deadlines defined above.

A player does not have until Week 8 merely because a normal pick remains unresolved.

### Selected puppy death

If the stud owner has already made a valid Puppy Back selection and the selected puppy later dies before transfer:

If:

- the puppy is still younger than the canonical Day-56 cutoff; and
- another qualifying puppy remains;

the stud owner's selection may reopen.

The original contract remains unchanged:

- same sire;
- same dam;
- same First/Second Pick contract;
- same required puppy sex;
- same linked litter;
- same prior dam protected-pick result.

The dam does not receive another protected first pick.

The reopened stud selection uses a new player-action deadline, but it may never extend beyond the canonical Day-56 cutoff.

The effective reselection deadline is:

**the earlier of:**
- 24 real hours after reselection opens; or
- the canonical Day-56 cutoff.

The game never automatically selects the replacement.

If no qualifying replacement exists, or the Day-56 selection window has closed:

- Puppy Back becomes unfulfilled;
- no alternate sex is substituted;
- no cash substitution occurs;
- no contract error occurs;
- Return Service does not arise from the puppy's death.

---

## 30. Contract protection

Contract protection continues to derive from the outstanding selection right.

### During Second Pick dam turn

Every currently living selectable puppy in the linked litter is protected because the dam's protected first choice is unrestricted.

### During stud selection

Only puppies that could legally satisfy the stud owner's outstanding selection are protected.

For Second Pick:

- the dam's protected puppy is excluded.

### After stud puppy selection

The selected Puppy Back puppy remains protected as the contractual claim until that claim is later resolved under the selected-puppy transfer lifecycle.

### Deadline passage

Protection is based on persisted contract-selection state.

Merely reaching a deadline does not release protection until the authoritative lifecycle transition records the forfeiture or other resolution.

Natural mortality is never blocked by Stud Contract protection.

---

## 31. Return Service lifecycle

Return Service is not a second Stud Contract.

It is one unresolved entitlement arising from the original immutable Stud Contract.

A single original contract can produce at most one Return Service entitlement.

### No-litter Return Service

If the canonical breeding lifecycle determines that the breeding produced no litter:

- if No-Litter Return Service was offered, the original contract enters Return Service;
- otherwise the relevant contract obligation resolves without Return Service.

### Small-Litter Return Service

When a litter is created:

- count puppies born alive;
- evaluate the immutable Small-Litter Return Service threshold immediately.

Available thresholds remain:

- None
- 1 or fewer
- 2 or fewer
- 3 or fewer

If the live-born count satisfies the configured threshold:

- the original contract gains one Return Service entitlement.

Otherwise:

- no later event can create Small-Litter Return Service for that litter.

### CASH-only contracts

For a CASH-only contract:

when the litter is created:

- if Small-Litter Return Service is triggered, the original contract enters Return Service;
- otherwise the contract resolves.

No neonatal waiting period applies.

### Puppy Back contracts

For PUPPY_BACK or CASH_AND_PUPPY_BACK:

the Return Service outcome is independently settled from the no-litter/small-litter rules.

The Puppy Back obligation may continue afterward until it becomes:

- selected;
- forfeited;
- unfulfillable;
- otherwise completed under the later transfer lifecycle.

Puppy Back failure never creates an additional Return Service.

---

## 32. Return Service duration

A Return Service entitlement is valid for:

**60 real days**

The clock begins when the Return Service entitlement becomes available.

The expiration is a fixed real-world timestamp.

The 60-day deadline:

- does not pause;
- does not extend because either dog is temporarily unavailable;
- does not extend because the sire is in Stud Recovery;
- does not extend because Breeding Availability is turned off;
- does not extend because required testing has expired.

At expiration:

the Return Service is permanently resolved as expired.

---

## 33. Return Service dog and kennel identity

Return Service applies only to:

- the same sire;
- the same dam;
- the original sire-owning contracting kennel;
- the original dam-owning contracting kennel.

It does not transfer with either dog.

### Ownership change

If the sire changes kennels:

- any outstanding Return Service under that contract is permanently extinguished.

If the dam changes kennels:

- any outstanding Return Service under that contract is permanently extinguished.

Returning the dog to the original kennel later does not restore the extinguished entitlement.

The ownership-transfer event itself extinguishes the Return Service.

The game should notify both contracting kennels.

The notice reports the resolution; it is not the authority that causes it.

---

## 34. Death and permanent breeding ineligibility

If either required dog dies before Return Service is successfully exercised:

- the outstanding Return Service is permanently extinguished.

If either required dog becomes permanently ineligible for breeding:

- the Return Service is permanently extinguished.

Examples may include terminal reproductive outcomes already represented authoritatively by the game.

Do not infer permanent ineligibility from a temporary restriction.

### Temporary unavailability

The following do not extinguish Return Service:

- Breeding Inactive;
- Stud Recovery;
- temporary reproductive recovery;
- temporary veterinary restriction;
- temporarily expired required health testing;
- other curable/current-eligibility failures.

The Return Service remains alive until its fixed expiration unless a permanent terminating event occurs.

The 60-day clock continues during temporary unavailability.

---

## 35. Return Service exercise

The dam-owning contracting kennel may attempt to exercise an available Return Service.

Player-facing action:

**Attempt Return Service**

The action is available from the contract record when the entitlement is still alive.

### No reservation

Return Service:

- does not reserve the sire;
- does not receive priority over other breedings;
- does not bypass Stud Recovery.

A popular public stud may therefore be unavailable when the dam owner attempts the return breeding.

This is intentional player-to-player gameplay.

### Current eligibility

At every attempt, the server rechecks normal current breeding eligibility.

Examples include:

- sire alive;
- dam alive;
- correct original ownership;
- Breeding Availability;
- breeding age;
- Stud Recovery;
- dam reproductive cooldown/recovery;
- pregnancy state;
- pending emergency/veterinary restrictions;
- other canonical biological eligibility.

If eligibility fails:

- no BreedingAttempt is created;
- Return Service is not consumed;
- the player receives the actual reason.

Examples:

`The sire is in Stud Recovery due to a recent breeding.`

`The dam is not currently active for breeding.`

`The dam is not eligible because the required brucellosis test is no longer current.`

The player may attempt again before expiration if the issue is temporary.

---

## 36. Return Service and contractual requirements

Return Service is a continuation of the original immutable contract.

It is not a new application to the sire's current public Stud Offer.

Therefore:

- no current public Stud Offer is required;
- current public terms are irrelevant;
- later price changes are irrelevant;
- later Puppy Back changes are irrelevant;
- no new Manual Approval request occurs;
- the sire owner cannot decline the already-existing entitlement merely because they changed their mind.

However, immutability freezes the agreement terms, not the dogs' biological condition forever.

### Time-sensitive requirements

A time-sensitive health/safety requirement contained in the original contract must be current when the Return Service breeding is attempted where the game's canonical rules require current status.

Brucellosis is the primary example.

If the original immutable contract required brucellosis testing:

- the appropriate current test requirement must be satisfied when Return Service is exercised.

If it is not:

- the breeding attempt fails;
- the Return Service remains unused;
- the 60-day expiration continues to run.

The player is responsible for satisfying the requirement before expiration.

Do not rerun the contract as though applying to a newly published offer.

## 37. Return Service compensation and completion

Return Service provides:

**one future breeding attempt under the original accepted Stud Contract, using the same sire and same dam.**

The original stud-owner compensation is not paid again.

### Cash

If the original contract used CASH:

- the original stud payment already stands.

### Cash + Puppy Back

If the original contract used CASH_AND_PUPPY_BACK:

- the cash portion already stands;
- no second stud payment occurs.

### Puppy Back

No new compensation structure is negotiated.

### Ordinary costs

Normal non-stud compensation expenses associated with performing another breeding remain applicable.

### Consumption

Return Service is consumed only when the replacement:

`BreedingAttempt`

is successfully created.

A failed eligibility attempt does not consume it.

Once the Return Service BreedingAttempt is successfully created:

- the Return Service entitlement is permanently resolved as used;
- the original Stud Contract no longer has another Return Service available.

### No recursive Return Service

The replacement breeding can never create another Return Service from the original contract.

This remains true regardless of whether the return breeding later results in:

- no pregnancy;
- no litter;
- a small litter;
- neonatal deaths;
- later puppy deaths;
- reproductive emergency;
- another otherwise unfavorable reproductive outcome.

There is one Return Service at most.

## 38. Stud Recovery and availability warning

Return Service does not override Stud Recovery.

It provides:

- no reservation;
- no queue priority;
- no forced availability.

A sire owner may continue normal gameplay with the sire.

A Return Service may therefore expire unused if the required breeding cannot be successfully initiated within 60 real days.

Player-facing fine print:

**Stud availability:** A Return Service does not bypass Stud Recovery or other temporary breeding restrictions, and it does not reserve the sire. The sire owner is not required by the game to keep the sire continuously available. A Return Service can therefore expire unused if the required breeding cannot be completed within 60 real days.

## 39. Player obligations and contract risk

Stud Contracts enforce game rules, persisted terms, deadlines, eligibility, and contractual consequences.

They do not guarantee cooperative behavior between players.

Player-facing fine print:

**Player obligations:** Stud Contracts create game-enforced terms, deadlines, and eligibility rules, but the game does not guarantee cooperative player behavior. Player decisions—including selling a contracted dog, making a dog unavailable for breeding, or allowing a deadline to expire—can prevent some contractual benefits from being fulfilled.

This paragraph requires explicit acknowledgment before a player confirms an individual Stud Contract.

## 40. Required Stud Contract fine print

The Stud Contract Worksheet Review/Publish step and dam-owner contract confirmation should clearly disclose at least the following.

### Return Service terms

A Return Service, when provided by this contract, is valid for 60 real days and applies only to the same sire and dam under their original contracting kennels. If either dog changes kennels, dies, or becomes permanently ineligible for breeding, the Return Service ends. Temporary unavailability does not extend the expiration date.

### Health and availability

A Return Service does not bypass current biological or safety requirements. Required time-sensitive testing, including brucellosis when required by this contract, must be current when the return breeding occurs.

### One return only

Exercising a Return Service completes the guarantee when the new BreedingAttempt is successfully created. The return breeding does not generate another Return Service regardless of pregnancy or litter outcome.

### Stud availability

A Return Service does not bypass Stud Recovery or other temporary breeding restrictions, and it does not reserve the sire. The sire owner is not required by the game to keep the sire continuously available. A Return Service can therefore expire unused if the required breeding cannot be completed within 60 real days.

### Ownership warning

Selling or transferring either the sire or dam to another kennel permanently ends any outstanding Return Service under this contract.

### Litter-size warning

No-litter and Small-Litter Return Service outcomes are determined from puppies born alive at whelping. Neonatal or later puppy deaths do not reduce that contractual litter count and do not create a Return Service.

### Puppy Back warning

Puppy Back terms carry selection risk. Required sex unavailable, a prior protected pick consuming the only qualifying puppy, a missed selection deadline, or later death of a selected puppy does not independently create Return Service.

### Player obligations acknowledgment

Stud Contracts create game-enforced terms, deadlines, and eligibility rules, but the game does not guarantee cooperative player behavior. Player decisions—including selling a contracted dog, making a dog unavailable for breeding, or allowing a deadline to expire—can prevent some contractual benefits from being fulfilled.

The dam owner must explicitly acknowledge the Player Obligations paragraph before Confirm is enabled.

## 42. Return Service outcome versus contract completion

Return Service outcome and overall contract completion must not be conflated.

### CASH

If a litter is created:

- evaluate Small-Litter Return Service immediately from live-born count.

If Return Service does not trigger:

- CASH contract is resolved at litter creation.

If Return Service triggers:

- the original contract remains unresolved in Return Service.

### PUPPY_BACK / CASH_AND_PUPPY_BACK

At whelping:

- determine Return Service outcome from live-born count.

If Return Service does not trigger:

- that fact is final.

The contract may nevertheless remain unresolved because Puppy Back selection is still active.

If Return Service does trigger:

- the Return Service entitlement exists independently from the Puppy Back obligation.

Puppy selection then proceeds according to the original terms if its minimum live-born litter requirement was also met.

The game must not later create a second Return Service because Puppy Back fails.
